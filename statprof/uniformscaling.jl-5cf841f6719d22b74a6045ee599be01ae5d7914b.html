6 """
7 UniformScaling{T<:Number}
8
9 Generically sized uniform scaling operator defined as a scalar times
10 the identity operator, `λ*I`. Although without an explicit `size`, it
11 acts similarly to a matrix in many cases and includes support for some
12 indexing. See also [`I`](@ref).
13
14 !!! compat "Julia 1.6"
15 Indexing using ranges is available as of Julia 1.6.
16
17 # Examples
18 ```jldoctest
19 julia> J = UniformScaling(2.)
20 UniformScaling{Float64}
21 2.0*I
22
23 julia> A = [1. 2.; 3. 4.]
24 2×2 Matrix{Float64}:
25 1.0 2.0
26 3.0 4.0
27
28 julia> J*A
29 2×2 Matrix{Float64}:
30 2.0 4.0
31 6.0 8.0
32
33 julia> J[1:2, 1:2]
34 2×2 Matrix{Float64}:
35 2.0 0.0
36 0.0 2.0
37 ```
38 """
39 struct UniformScaling{T<:Number}
40 λ::T
41 end

357 nans::Bool = false, norm::Function = norm)
358 n = checksquare(A)
